0.18.49 · Safe area and external screen layout improvements
• New external screen safe area: direct access to the bottom of the homepage, independent automatic/manual in four directions, supporting positive and negative adjustments, auxiliary lines, recovery and reset.
• Unify the interface and touch boundaries, leaving the shortcut bar outside the safe area; fix the display area shrinking after the settings page is rotated or rebuilt.
• Fixed page rounded corners, bottom cropping and notification masks, and controlled the horizontal rotation of the center to maintain the tool sidebar.
• Improved white strip and border synchronization and copyable diagnosis; display the homepage authorization entrance when accessibility is not enabled.

0.18.40 · Stability and status feedback
• Window information is read in the background to reduce service startup delays; the application center search and task pages maintain the current page when the keyboard and margins change.
• The control center typesets text according to the actual density of the external screen, leaving space for icons to avoid system font settings from crowding the layout.
• Swipe the notification card right by more than 30% to request clearing; the status bar displays the VPN reported by the system, and the application directory is updated with the activation status.
